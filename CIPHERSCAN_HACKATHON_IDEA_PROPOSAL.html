Domain: Cybersecurity & Mobile Safety
⚠️ The Problem

Mobile users are targeted through SMS (smishing), messaging apps (WhatsApp, Telegram), and QR codes (quishing). Mobile devices are extremely vulnerable due to:

  • Hidden Destinations: Mobile address bars truncate long, deceptive URLs.
  • No Hover Previews: Tap-to-execute links expose users instantly without any preview.
  • UPI Payment Fraud: Manipulated payment links (upi://pay) trick users into sending money under disguised merchant names.
  • Zero-Day Redirects: Shortened and disposable links evade traditional static domain blacklists.
💡 The Solution: CipherScan

CipherScan functions as a "digital bomb-defusal squad" for links. It intercepts dangerous links before your browser or payment app touches them:

  • Zero-Exposure Interception: Catches web and UPI links at the Android OS intent level the exact moment they are tapped.
  • Headless Cloud Sandbox: Safely executes the link in a remote cloud container to inspect redirects, scripts, and visual previews.
  • Multi-Engine Intelligence: Cross-checks with 70+ threat feeds, Google Safe Browsing, and payment anomaly heuristics.
  • Instant Shield Card: Displays a clear risk score (Safe / Suspicious / Dangerous) before allowing access.
⚡ How It Works (End-to-End Pipeline)
1
Tap / Scan
User clicks link in SMS, WhatsApp, or scans QR
2
OS Intercept
CipherScan halts launch before browser opens
3
Cloud Detonation
Headless Chromium sandbox traces 301/302 redirects
4
Multi-Threat Check
Scans 70+ Antivirus feeds & UPI payment parameters
5
Safety Verdict
Visual snapshot & risk score shown; safe links launch
🚀 Unique Selling Propositions (USPs) & Key Features

🔒 Pre-Execution Defense

Unlike browser extensions, CipherScan stops threats at the Android OS layer across any app (SMS, WhatsApp, Mail, QR).

📸 Visual Screenshot Sandbox

Captures live webpage screenshots in an isolated cloud sandbox so users see what the page looks like without visiting it.

💳 UPI Fraud Protection

Specially parses UPI deep-links (VPA address, merchant name, amount limits) to detect QR payment swapping scams.

⚡ Sub-Second Smart Cache

24-hour intelligent caching deduplicates previously scanned safe links for instant, zero-lag link opening.

📊 Live Security Dashboard

Web management portal for enterprise or individual telemetry, monitoring scan history and threat distributions.

🛡️ Battery & Privacy Friendly

No battery-draining continuous VPN background filters; processes only clicked links on-demand.

🎯 Target Audience & Use Cases

  • UPI & Digital Payment Users: Preventing fraudulent merchant QR code scams at retail outlets.
  • Enterprises & BYOD: Mobile threat monitoring for corporate mobile devices without intrusive device tracking.
